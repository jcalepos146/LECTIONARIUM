## Quicunque vult
=191
A: O Holy and Undivided Trinity.
S: Whosoever will be saved : before all things it is necessary that he hold the Catholick Faith.
S: Which Faith except every one do keep whole and undefiled : without doubt he shall perish everlastingly.
S: And the Catholick Faith is this : That we worship one God in Trinity, and Trinity in Unity;
S: Neither confounding the Persons : nor dividing the Substance.
S: For there is one Person of the Father, another of the Son : and another of the Holy Ghost.
S: But the Godhead of the Father, of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost, is all One : the Glory equal, the Majesty co-eternal.
S: Such as the Father is, such is the Son : and such is the Holy Ghost.
S: The Father uncreate, the Son uncreate : and the Holy Ghost uncreate.
S: The Father incomprehensible, the Son incomprehensible : and the Holy Ghost incomprehensible.
S: The Father eternal, the Son eternal : and the Holy Ghost eternal.
S: And yet They are not three eternals : but One Eternal.
S: As also there are not three incomprehensibles, nor three uncreated : but One Uncreated, and One Incomprehensible.
S: So likewise the Father is Almighty, the Son Almighty : and the Holy Ghost Almighty.
S: And yet They are not three almighties : but One Almighty.
S: So the Father is God, the Son is God : and the Holy Ghost is God.
S: And yet They are not three gods : but One God.
S: So likewise the Father is Lord, the Son Lord : and the Holy Ghost Lord.
S: And yet not three lords : but One Lord.
S: For like as we are compelled by the Christian Verity : to acknowledge every Person by Himself to be God and Lord;
S: So are we forbidden by the Catholick Religion : to say, There be three gods, or three lords.
S: The Father is made of none : neither created, nor begotten.
S: The Son is of the Father alone : not made, nor created, but begotten.
S: The Holy Ghost is of the Father and of the Son : neither made, nor created, nor begotten, but Proceeding.
S: So there is one Father, not three Fathers; one Son, not three Sons : one Holy Ghost, not three Holy Ghosts.
S: And in this Trinity none is afore, or after other : none is greater, or less than another;
S: But the whole Three Persons are co-eternal together : and co-equal.
=192
S: So that in all things, as is aforesaid : the Unity in Trinity, and the Trinity in Unity is to be worshipped.
S: He therefore that will be saved : must thus think of the Trinity.
S: Furthermore, it is necessary to everlasting Salvation : that he also believe rightly the Incarnation of our Lord Jesus Christ.
S: For the right Faith is, that we believe and confess : that our Lord Jesus Christ, the Son of God, is God and Man.
S: God, of the substance of the Father, begotten before the worlds : and man of the substance of His Mother, born in the world;
S: Perfect God, and perfect Man : of a reasonable soul and human flesh subsisting;
S: Equal to the Father, as touching His Godhead : and Inferior to the Father, as touching His Manhood.
S: Who although He be God and Man : yet He is not two, but One Christ;
S: One; not by conversion of the Godhead into flesh : but by taking of the Manhood into God.
S: One altogether; not by confusion of Substance : but by unity of Person.
S: For as the reasonable soul and flesh is one man : so God and Man is One Christ;
S: Who suffered for our salvation : descended into hell, rose again the third day from the dead.
S: He ascended into heaven, He sitteth on the right hand of the Father, God Almighty : from whence He shall come to judge the quick and the dead.
S: At Whose coming all men shall rise again with their bodies : and shall give account for their own works.
S: And they that have done good shall go into life everlasting : and they that have done evil into everlasting fire.
S: This is the Catholick Faith : which except a man believe faithfully, he cannot be saved.
